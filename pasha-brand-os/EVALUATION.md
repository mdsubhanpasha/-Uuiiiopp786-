# EVALUATION.md — Virality Scorer Pearson Correlation Accuracy Benchmark

## Overview
This document evaluates the accuracy and predictive performance of the **Node 4 Critic & Virality Scorer** engine within `PASHA-UNIFIED-OS`. The objective is to verify that the 0-100 Virality Score generated by GPT-4o (scoring Hook Strength, Value, Authenticity, and CTA) correlates strongly with actual organic LinkedIn view metrics (`Pearson correlation r > 0.85`).

---

## Methodology & Dataset
We compiled a benchmark dataset of 20 past viral tech executive LinkedIn posts covering core domains: Voice AI, Stateful Multi-Agent LangGraph, Qdrant Hybrid RAG, DALL-E 3 visual carousels, Telegram approval loops, and Prometheus observability telemetry.

Each post was evaluated by the Virality Scorer to assign a score (0-100) and matched against actual scraped 6-hour LinkedIn view metrics.
